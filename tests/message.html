Welcome to the demo

This page shows the global message ticker in action. The ticker reads messages from MESSAGE_DATA in messageData.js and scrolls them from right to left.

✨ Features:

  • Scrolls messages smoothly from right → left
  • Each message disappears at the left edge
  • Automatic cycling through all messages
  • Close button (✕) – remembers your choice
  • Works in any HTML page – just add <div id="messageView"></div>
  • Fully responsive & customizable

💡 To reopen the ticker after closing, open DevTools and run: reopenMessageTicker()
